Go to the login page using the provided link and enter the username and password, then click Sign In.
You will be directed to the Admission Forms page, where you will see an application with the status Incomplete. Click Edit to open the application form.

"I have read and understood this applicant declaration, and if admitted to the University of Calgary, I agree to comply with all rules and regulations of the University."

"I will pay by credit card. (Visa/MasterCard/American Express; Visa/MasterCard Debit)"
